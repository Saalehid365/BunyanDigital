    Revenue Opportunity Scorecard

  3. 03

Who qualifies

Free, for the right stores.

To keep it free without wasting your time or ours, the sprint is for stores that meet these. 4 spots per month, capped at what we can deliver well.

  • Are live on Shopify or WooCommerce, or sell on Amazon or eBay
  • Do at least ~50 orders a month, or ~£3,000 in monthly revenue
  • Are based in the UK
  • Have the decision-maker on the reveal call
  • Can give read-only or collaborator access (store admin, GA4, Meta Ads) within 48 hours
