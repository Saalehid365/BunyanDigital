The offer

Money found, not homework.

A free, qualified sprint where we find 3 specific revenue opportunities in your live store within 7 working days. Each one comes with an estimated £ value and a plan to capture it.

You don't get a list of what's wrong. You get what you stand to gain, and exactly how to get it.

Four deliverables, all free.

    Revenue Opportunity Scorecard

    An 8 to 12 minute screen recording showing each opportunity on your own store, so you see it rather than read about it.

Who qualifies

Free, for the right stores.

To keep it free without wasting your time or ours, the sprint is for stores that meet these. 4 spots per month, capped at what we can deliver well.

  • Are live on Shopify or WooCommerce, or sell on Amazon or eBay
  • Do at least ~50 orders a month, or ~£3,000 in monthly revenue
  • Are based in the UK
  • Have the decision-maker on the reveal call
  • Can give read-only or collaborator access (store admin, GA4, Meta Ads) within 48 hours
