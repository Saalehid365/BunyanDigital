Do I need a new phone number?

No. We connect your existing WhatsApp Business number. During setup, it moves to the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. We write your assistant's replies in your tone, and a native speaker checks every Arabic reply.

What if a customer asks something it can't answer?

It passes the chat to you or your team straight away, so no customer is left without a reply.

Is my customers' data safe?

We follow Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and only use your data to run your assistant.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are monthly, and you can cancel at any time.
