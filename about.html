About

Bunyan means “to build”. That's the whole job.

We're a UK ecommerce agency for brands that want a store that performs in a crowded market, and a growth engine behind it.

Why we started.

Bunyan Digital started with a simple observation. Too many ecommerce brands had storefronts and marketing that looked the part but didn't convert: good-looking themes with no strategy behind them.

We work the other way round. We build around WhatsApp as a sales channel, content that converts, and the platforms and marketplaces where your customers already spend: Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon and eBay. The same team runs every engagement from strategy to execution, with no handoffs and no scope creep.
