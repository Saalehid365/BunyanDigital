✳ About Us

Bunyan means "to build." That's the whole job.

We're an ecommerce agency based in the United Kingdom and Madinah, Saudi Arabia, built for brands who want a store — and a growth engine — that actually performs in the Saudi market.

Our Story

Built in the Kingdom, for the Kingdom.

Bunyan Digital started with a simple observation: most ecommerce brands in Saudi Arabia were using storefronts and marketing playbooks built for Western shoppers, translated rather than truly localized.

We build differently — around Mada and STC Pay, around WhatsApp as a sales channel, around Arabic-first content, and around the marketplaces (Noon, Amazon.sa, Salla) where GCC shoppers actually spend.

Every engagement is run by the same team from strategy through to execution — no handoffs, no scope creep, just a store that sells.
